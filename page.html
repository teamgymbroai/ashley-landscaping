What he builds.

Most jobs start with getting the ground right. Everything else sits on top of that.

{{IMG:s-ground|Ground levelled and framed with new sleepers before paving.|340px}}

01Levelling and groundworks

Levelling slopes, cutting into banks, taking out turf and topsoil, then hardcore, chippings and edging.

{{IMG:s-patio|A large Indian sandstone patio running the length of a brick house, edged with brick soldier courses.|340px}}

02Patios and paving

Indian sandstone, porcelain, brick and setts. New patios, and recovery on paving that has dropped.

{{IMG:s-path|A reclaimed brick circle path meeting a sandstone terrace across a sleeper step.|340px}}

03Paths and driveways

Drives remodelled, entrances widened, and paths laid around existing trees.

{{IMG:s-sleep|Two tiers of dark stained sleeper raised beds, newly planted, beside a gravel path.|340px}}

04Sleeper walls and raised beds

Retaining walls that hold a level, and beds rebuilt in new sleepers where decking planks rotted out.

{{IMG:s-fence|A curved sandstone patio with a reclaimed brick soldier edge, against a new close boarded fence.|340px}}

05Fencing, gates and screens

Close boarded fencing, arris rails, front gates to a drive, and Venetian slatted screening.

{{IMG:s-struct|A sandstone terrace with a granite sett border laid around a timber garden room.|340px}}

06Pergolas and structures

Pergolas, trellis and fruit frames, and the paving that sits around a garden room.
