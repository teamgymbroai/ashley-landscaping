What he builds.

Every line here comes from something a customer described in a review.

01Levelling and groundworks

Regrading, cutting into banks, turf and topsoil out, hardcore, chippings and edging in.

02Patios and paving

Indian sandstone, porcelain, brick and setts. New patios, and recovery on paving that has dropped.

03Paths and driveways

Drives remodelled, entrances widened, and paths laid around existing trees.

04Sleeper walls and raised beds

Retaining walls that hold a level, and beds rebuilt in new sleepers where decking planks rotted out.

05Fencing, gates and screens

Close boarded fencing, arris rails, front gates to a drive, and Venetian slatted screening.

06Pergolas and structures

Pergolas, trellis and fruit frames, and the paving that sits around a garden room.

07Hedging and clearance

Hedges out and replaced, tree removal, and gardens that have got away from their owners.
